## 🎮 2. Best Gaming Experience Guide

To get the ultimate "console-level" gaming experience, we highly recommend using iEmulator like this:

1. **Connect a Wireless Controller**: Go to your iPhone or iPad's "Settings -> Bluetooth" to connect your wireless controller (such as a PS5, Xbox controller, etc.). Once connected, iEmulator will automatically recognize and adapt to the controller, giving you the best control experience.
2. **Immersive Big Screen Mirroring**: Use "Screen Mirroring" from the system's top drop-down menu to cast your screen to an Apple TV, iMac, MacBook, or other Apple devices.
   > 💡 **Special Note**: iEmulator is exclusively optimized for screen mirroring! The screen mirroring projects **only the pure game footage**, without the app interface or virtual buttons, bringing you a true immersive big-screen experience.
3. **Native Big Screen Gaming**: If you are using a large-screen iPad, you don't need to mirror the screen. Just grab your wireless controller and enjoy perfect big-screen gaming directly.
